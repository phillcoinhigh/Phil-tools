The Phil tour, runnable

Phil's own tour explains the language in twenty sections. This page follows it section by section. Where the tour shows a piece of a program, this page has a whole one you can run and change, in your browser, on any device.

Read the tour for the explanation; come here to try it. Each section links to the original.

The section titles are from A Tour of Phil in Phil's repository. The summaries and the programs are ours, and they run in our simulator, which is a model of Phil and not Phil's compiler. This is a community project and is not affiliated with Phil.
