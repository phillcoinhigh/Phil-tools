Philcoin
Who we are

    

      
A community, not a company
Philcoin is a community token. The people who hold it like Phil and want it to succeed, so we build things for it in the open.

      
Free for everyone
Nothing here needs the token. There are no accounts, nothing is gated, and all of the code is open under Apache-2.0.

      
Independent of Phil
We are not the Phil project and do not speak for it. Phil is the work of its author, and the token gives no rights in it. Nothing here is financial advice.
